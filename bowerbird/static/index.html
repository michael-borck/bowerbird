Ask your bookmarks

Ask a question — Bower finds saved links that might help. It never answers from the web; every result is something you bookmarked.

Domain triage

One decision retires a whole domain. “Let go” marks every unreviewed link on the domain retired (applied at write-back, reversible until then); “Keep” records them as deliberate.

Topic map

Your collection's themes — node size is bookmark count, links join topics that share bookmarks. Click a topic to browse it in the Library.

Graveyard

“Replace” swaps the link for its Wayback snapshot, “Let go” retires it — both are recorded decisions applied at write-back, never silently. Run bowerbird wayback to look up snapshots, and bowerbird writeback --source-id 1 for a dry-run of the changes.

Duplicates

Duplicates are within one browser only — the same link saved in two browsers is intentional. The kept copy is the one filed most specifically; collapsing happens at write-back (Phase 4), nothing is removed yet.

Rediscover

Bookmarks you saved and (as far as your browsers' history knows) never went back to. One domain each.
